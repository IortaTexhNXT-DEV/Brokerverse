Process flow 01 · BDOIR E2E PROCESS FLOW.pdf

BDOIR End-to-End

The umbrella flow that chains new business, cashiering, collections, remittance, disbursement, the general ledger, renewal and claims. Each link is captured in detail in its own process below; these screens show the control points that tie the chain together.

Personas in this walkthrough: admin

  1. 01.01

    Executive dashboard: KPIs across the whole chain (in force, in placement, unapplied payments, pending approvals, disbursements in flight, past-TAT requests)

    Screen: Dashboard

  2. 01.02

    Single maker-checker queue for bookings, endorsements, journals, disbursements, CTE, claim settlements, EB awards and product changes

    Screen: Approvals & Audit › All

  3. 01.03

    Production, collection and claims reports with CSV export

    Screen: Reports & Analytics

  4. 01.04

    Client onboarding with sanctions screening, PEP flag and risk tiering feeding every process

    Screen: Sanction Screening & Risk

  5. 01.05

    Twelve personas with module entitlements and approver rights (segregation of duties)

    Screen: User Access Maintenance

  6. 01.06

    Legacy data migration batches with load, reconciliation and disposition

    Screen: Data Migration

Process flow 02 · NEW BUSINESS.pdf

New Business

Client creation and screening, quotation, proposal, placement to the insurer, e-policy and booking under maker-checker.

Personas in this walkthrough: nb.officer → uw.head

  1. 02.01

    MAO creates the client in Broker; KYC details captured

    Screen: Sanction Screening & Risk › New client form

  2. 02.02

    Client is sanction-screened automatically (clear / hit) and risk-tiered

    Screen: Sanction Screening & Risk › Client register

    Screening result and CDD level shown on the row

  3. 02.03

    Quotation prepared with the rating engine (premium, VAT, DST, LGT, commission)

    Screen: New Business › Quotations & proposals › New quotation form

  4. 02.04

    Quotation created (status: quoted); hold cover can be requested from the insurer

    Screen: New Business › Quotations & proposals

  5. 02.05

    Proposal sent to the client by email (status: proposal sent)

    Screen: New Business › Quotations & proposals › Send proposal

  6. 02.06

    Client acceptance recorded (Y); a decline with reason is the N branch

    Screen: New Business › Quotations & proposals › Client accepted

  7. 02.07

    Placement requested: placement slip goes to the insurer via SFTP or email

    Screen: New Business › Policy drawer (placement requested)

  8. 02.08

    Placement board tracks slips awaiting the insurer, returns and e-policies

    Screen: New Business › Placement

  9. 02.09

    Insurer placed the risk; e-policy received (COG via SFTP or PO email)

    Screen: New Business › Placement › Placed / e-policy received

  10. 02.10

    Booking and invoicing raised for checker approval (maker-checker)

    Screen: New Business › Placement › Book & invoice

  11. 02.11

    Underwriting Head (checker) reviews the booking request

    Screen: Approvals & Audit › Pending

  12. 02.12

    Booking approved: invoice, journal and e-policy dispatch are posted automatically

    Screen: Approvals & Audit › Approve

  13. 02.13

    Policy register: the policy is in force with its invoice and e-policy channel

    Screen: New Business › Policies

  14. 02.14

    Policy detail: placement history, e-policy status and the exception route to the contact centre

    Screen: New Business › Policies › Policy detail

Process flow 03 · OPERATIONS PROCESS FLOW.pdf

Operations

Cashiering channels, post-dated cheque hold, unapplied premium, direct payments with commission receivables, adjustments and production reconciliation.

Personas in this walkthrough: cashier → checker

  1. 03.01

    Cashiering: open invoices (premium receivables) awaiting payment

    Screen: Operations › Invoices & cashiering

  2. 03.02

    Receive payment: channel (bills payment, OTC cash/cheque, batch files, autopay) and reference

    Screen: Operations › Invoices & cashiering › Receive

  3. 03.03

    Cheque payment recorded and held for the 4-day clearing period

    Screen: Operations › Receive › Record payment

  4. 03.04

    PDC register: held cheques, matured, bounced (exclusion list) and unapplied premium

    Screen: Operations › Payments / PDC / UPP

  5. 03.05

    Held cheque shows Matured / Bounced actions once the 4-day hold lapses

    Screen: Operations › Payments / PDC / UPP › Held cheque

  6. 03.06

    Cash / bills payment applied in full: official receipt issued and receivable cleared

    Screen: Operations › Receive › Official receipt

  7. 03.07

    Payment with zero PR / payment greater than PR is recorded without an invoice

    Screen: Operations › Invoices & cashiering › Payment without invoice

  8. 03.08

    The amount sits in Unapplied Premium (GL 2400) until applied to an invoice

    Screen: Operations › Payments / PDC / UPP

  9. 03.09

    Direct payment to insurer identified for a booked policy

    Screen: Operations › Direct payments › Identify

  10. 03.10

    Commission receivable (GL 1250) billed to the insurer; follow-up until collected

    Screen: Operations › Direct payments › Bill insurer

  11. 03.11

    Commission collected: commission official receipt issued

    Screen: Operations › Direct payments › Collect (commission OR)

  12. 03.12

    Adjustment / cancellation validated by the Adjustment Team and routed to the checker-poster

    Screen: Operations › Adjustments › Endorsement form

  13. 03.13

    Endorsement pending checker approval; a refund creates a Refund Request Form (RRF) on approval

    Screen: Operations › Adjustments

  14. 03.14

    Production reconciliation: insurer production file matched against booked policies

    Screen: Operations › Production recon › Upload

  15. 03.15

    Rows classified as matched, with discrepancies, or unbooked; each gets a disposition

    Screen: Operations › Production recon › Result

  16. 03.16

    Checker-poster reviews the endorsement

    Screen: Approvals & Audit › Pending

  17. 03.17

    Endorsement posted: premium and commission reversed, RRF raised for the refund

    Screen: Approvals & Audit › Approve
