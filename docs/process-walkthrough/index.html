BDOIR End-to-End

The umbrella flow that chains new business, cashiering, collections, remittance, disbursement, the general ledger, renewal and claims. Each link is captured in detail in its own process below; these screens show the control points that tie the chain together.

Personas in this walkthrough: admin · 6 steps

  1. Executive dashboard: KPIs across the whole chain (in force, in placement, unapplied payments, pending approvals, disbursements in flight, past-TAT requests)
  2. Single maker-checker queue for bookings, endorsements, journals, disbursements, CTE, claim settlements, EB awards and product changes
  3. Production, collection and claims reports with CSV export
  4. Client onboarding with sanctions screening, PEP flag and risk tiering feeding every process
  5. Twelve personas with module entitlements and approver rights (segregation of duties)
  6. Legacy data migration batches with load, reconciliation and disposition

Process flow 02 · NEW BUSINESS.pdf

New Business

Client creation and screening, quotation, proposal, placement to the insurer, e-policy and booking under maker-checker.

Personas in this walkthrough: nb.officer → uw.head · 14 steps

  1. MAO creates the client in Broker; KYC details captured
  2. Client is sanction-screened automatically (clear / hit) and risk-tiered
  3. Quotation prepared with the rating engine (premium, VAT, DST, LGT, commission)
  4. Quotation created (status: quoted); hold cover can be requested from the insurer
  5. Proposal sent to the client by email (status: proposal sent)
  6. Client acceptance recorded (Y); a decline with reason is the N branch
  7. Placement requested: placement slip goes to the insurer via SFTP or email
  8. Placement board tracks slips awaiting the insurer, returns and e-policies
  9. Insurer placed the risk; e-policy received (COG via SFTP or PO email)
  10. Booking and invoicing raised for checker approval (maker-checker)
  11. Underwriting Head (checker) reviews the booking request
  12. Booking approved: invoice, journal and e-policy dispatch are posted automatically
  13. Policy register: the policy is in force with its invoice and e-policy channel
  14. Policy detail: placement history, e-policy status and the exception route to the contact centre

Process flow 03 · OPERATIONS PROCESS FLOW.pdf

Operations

Cashiering channels, post-dated cheque hold, unapplied premium, direct payments with commission receivables, adjustments and production reconciliation.

Personas in this walkthrough: cashier → checker · 17 steps

  1. Cashiering: open invoices (premium receivables) awaiting payment
  2. Receive payment: channel (bills payment, OTC cash/cheque, batch files, autopay) and reference
  3. Cheque payment recorded and held for the 4-day clearing period
  4. PDC register: held cheques, matured, bounced (exclusion list) and unapplied premium
  5. Held cheque shows Matured / Bounced actions once the 4-day hold lapses
  6. Cash / bills payment applied in full: official receipt issued and receivable cleared
  7. Payment with zero PR / payment greater than PR is recorded without an invoice
  8. The amount sits in Unapplied Premium (GL 2400) until applied to an invoice
  9. Direct payment to insurer identified for a booked policy
  10. Commission receivable (GL 1250) billed to the insurer; follow-up until collected
  11. Commission collected: commission official receipt issued
  12. Adjustment / cancellation validated by the Adjustment Team and routed to the checker-poster
  13. Endorsement pending checker approval; a refund creates a Refund Request Form (RRF) on approval
  14. Production reconciliation: insurer production file matched against booked policies
  15. Rows classified as matched, with discrepancies, or unbooked; each gets a disposition
  16. Checker-poster reviews the endorsement
  17. Endorsement posted: premium and commission reversed, RRF raised for the refund
