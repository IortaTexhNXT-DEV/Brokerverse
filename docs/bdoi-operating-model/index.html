How BDOI works, end to end

BDOI stands between BDO’s clients and the insurance market. It onboards and screens the client, quotes and places the risk with an insurer, books the policy and invoices the premium, collects it, keeps its commission and remits the net premium to the insurer, accounts for every peso, services the client, handles claims, and renews the cover before it expires. The sixteen signed-off process flows in HL_Process_Overview are the pieces; this page shows them as one machine.

The whole chain on one picture

Read left to right. Each lane is a unit or counterparty; each column is a stage. Gold boxes are the client’s own steps. Dashed arrows are conditional branches: a non-packaged risk goes to TSU, a large risk to reinsurance, a claim is accepted only when the premium is paid.

Where the money goes

A broker touches money four ways: it collects premium on the insurer’s behalf, keeps its commission, remits the rest, and returns what is over-paid. Claims money never passes through BDOI; the insurer pays the assured or the repair shop directly.

The life of one policy

The clocks the flows prescribe, laid on one line: how fast a booked premium must be collected, how long cover runs, and when renewal starts.

Stage by stage

Twelve stages cover the chain. Each card names the units, what starts the stage, what happens, what it produces, the control that guards it, the clock, the source flows, and the BrokerVerse screen where it runs.

Quotation & proposal

MAO · Marketing TL · TSU (non-packaged) · Reinsurance

Starts when Screened client requests cover, or a renewal/conversion opportunity is opened.

  • Packaged product: rating engine prices premium, VAT, DST, LGT, FST and commission from the product table
  • Non-packaged risk: TSU request → completeness and duplicate check → quotation slip → TL approval → RI referral if needed → sent to insurers → responses with evidence → comparative table → proposal slip approved
  • Proposal emailed; client accepts or declines with reason; hold cover can be requested from the insurer
Produces
Quotation (QT-no.), proposal, TSU proposal slip, hold-cover request
Control
Non-packaged products cannot be quoted without an approved TSU proposal; acceptance limits and survey threshold checked at quotation.
Clock
TSU SLA escalation on breach; insurer follow-up until all quotes are in

Cashiering & payment application

Cashiering Team · UPP handler · Marketing (direct payments)

Starts when Client pays: bills payment, OTC cash or cheque, CLPC / PMS / trade batch files, direct credit, autopay, or directly to the insurer.

  • Payment applied to the invoice; official receipt issued; cash and premium journals posted
  • Post-dated / OTC cheques held 4 days, then matured and applied, or tagged bounced (exclusion list)
  • Zero-PR or excess payments become unapplied premium (GL 2400) until matched
  • Direct payment to insurer: commission receivable (GL 1250) identified → billed → approved → collected with a commission OR
Produces
Official receipt (OR-no.), PDC register, unapplied list, commission billing
Control
Cheques cannot mature before the clearing date; unapplied premium never sits against an invoice.
Clock
4-day cheque hold

Remittance & disbursement

Operations (extract) · Disbursement DPO → DTL → DSH → DUH · Insurer

Starts when Applied payments accumulate; weekly cycle (Monday to Friday coverage).

  • The same chain pays refunds, suppliers, reimbursements and cash advances
Produces
Remittance schedule, disbursement voucher, payment journal, confirmation email
Control
Requester, reviewer, approver and payer are different people; nothing is paid without the approval chain.
Clock
Weekly extract; end-of-day processing

Adjustments, cancellations & refunds

Adjustment Team · Operations checker-poster · MAO · Marketing TL · Unit Head

Starts when Client requests a change or cancellation; production register shows a discrepancy; unapplied premium to be returned.

  • Endorsement (adjustment or cancellation) validated and posted under checker-poster; premium delta journaled; cancellation sets the policy cancelled

  • Refund Request Form raised from the endorsement or UPP → Marketing TL review → Unit Head sign-off → refund disbursement voucher
Produces
Endorsement (END-no.), production recon run, RRF and refund voucher
Control
Refunds only originate from endorsements or unapplied premium and are paid only through the disbursement chain.

10

Claims

Claim handler · Insurer / adjuster · Assured · Casa / dealer

Starts when Client, account officer or branch sends notice of loss.

  • Claims Acceptance Control checks the policy is in force and premium paid; Preliminary Loss Advice sent
  • Document checklist by line (motor; non-motor via Claims Reporting Form); Formal Loss Advice to the insurer once complete; adjuster flagged
  • Insurer evaluates and offers; assured accepts or contests (re-evaluation loop, more documents may be requested)
  • Settlement by Letter of Authority to the preferred casa/dealer or by cash cheque; settlement approved; claim tagged settled and closed, or declined with reason
Produces
PLA, FLA, offer, LOA, settlement
Control
FLA blocked until documents complete; settlement is maker-checker; every stage has a declination exit.
Clock
Age in days tracked per claim

Renewal (automated RMEL)

Starts when Policy enters the 140-day window before expiry.

  • Bank-submitted policies feed the sanitation handler 150 days from expiry (conversion opportunity)

Satellite processes and where they plug in

Four flows run beside the main chain and feed it at specific stages.

Facultative reinsurance

Feeds stage 2–3 for large or special risks referred by TSU: request → acknowledge (24 h) → underwriting information loop → slip with reinsurers, security rating and signed-slip evidence (3 working days) → closing and debit note to the cedant. RI commission is monitored with the Commission Receivable Unit.

RENISURANCE · step-by-step

Employee benefits

A parallel new-business and renewal track for group schemes: renewal advice → Broker of Record → Terms of Reference with master list and utilisation released to insurers → comparative analysis (premium, benefits, capabilities) → client confirmation and award, with ISACOM approval for a non-accredited provider → handoff to processing and collections (stages 4–6).

EMPLOYEE BENEFITS · step-by-step

Submitted policies

Policies the client bought elsewhere and submitted to the bank for its loan: masterlist validated, matched and consolidated; adequacy reviewed; findings issue an Insurance Adequacy Assessment Form; accounts 150 days from expiry go to the sanitation handler as renewal or conversion opportunities (stage 12).

Submitted Policies · step-by-step

Product maintenance

Keeps stage 2 honest: package creation or change requests are validated, approved under maker-checker and published as a release advisory to Marketing, Operations and TSU; the insurer panel records accreditation (drives ISACOM) and SFTP enrolment (drives the placement channel).

How the processes hand off to each other

The integration points. Each is a document or a state that one process writes and the next one reads.

Onboarding → QuotationOnly a screened client can be quoted; the client number carries into every later document.
Quotation → TSU → ReinsuranceNon-packaged risks leave New Business for a TSU quotation slip; large or special risks are referred to RI before quoting; the approved proposal slip returns to New Business.
Placement → Booking → CashieringPlacement creates the policy; the checker’s approval creates the invoice; cashiering works only from invoices.
Cashiering → CollectionsUnpaid invoices become the PR list; applied payments leave it. Beyond-term commitments raise a CTE for the Finance Head.
Cashiering → Remittance → Disbursement → InsurerApplied premium is extracted weekly, sanitised and paid to insurers through the four-signature chain; direct-paid policies are excluded.
Endorsement → Refund → DisbursementA cancellation or adjustment with a refund raises an RRF; TL review and Unit Head sign-off create the refund voucher.
Everything → FRBS ledgerBooking, receipts, commissions, remittances, refunds and settlements post journals automatically; manual entries and ACSL adjustments post only after review and approval.
Collections → ClaimsClaims Acceptance Control reads the invoice balance: an unpaid premium blocks registration.
Booking → Renewal → PlacementIn-force policies enter the RMEL 140 days before expiry; an accepted renewal re-enters placement and booking.
Submitted policies → RenewalAdequacy-reviewed bank-submitted policies feed the sanitation handler 150 days before expiry.
Contact centre → Fulfilment unitsAccount-related cases are routed to the unit that owns the account; returns go back to the CCC for re-logging.
Product maintenance → New Business / TSURelease advisories tell Marketing, Operations and TSU what changed in a package and from when.
